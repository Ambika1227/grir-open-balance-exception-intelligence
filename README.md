**GRIR Open Balance \& Exception Intelligence**

**Project Overview**

This project analyses Goods Receipt/Invoice Receipt (GRIR) open items to identify outstanding balances, ageing exposure, vendor concentration, and reasons for unresolved items.



The objective is to help Finance, Accounts Payable, Procurement, and Receiving teams prioritize follow-ups and resolve long-pending GRIR items.



**Business Objectives**

* Monitor total outstanding GRIR balances.
* Identify items pending for more than 90 and 180 days.
* Analyze outstanding amounts by vendor and company code.
* Identify common reasons for unresolved GRIR items.
* Prioritize old and high-value items for follow-up.

**Tools Used**

* Microsoft Power BI — dashboard development and visual analysis
* MySQL — data validation, aggregation, and analysis
* Microsoft Excel — dataset review

**Key Findings**

* Total open balance: approximately ₹5.06 crore.
* Total open items: 500.
* Average ageing: 207 days.
* Items older than 90 days: 402 (80.4%).
* Outstanding amount older than 90 days: approximately ₹4.12 crore.
* Amount in the 180+ days ageing bucket: approximately ₹2.88 crore.
